About IEEE TEMS

TEMS stands for Technology and Engineering Management Society. Our student chapter is built for learners who want to understand how technology projects are planned, led and turned into real products.

You do not need prior experience. If you are curious about how great ideas become successful ventures, you belong here.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can join the chapter?

Any student who is interested in technology and how it is managed. Prior experience is not required.

Will this help my career?

Yes. Leadership, teamwork and project experience stand out on a resume and in interviews.

How much time does it need?

Only a few hours a month. Take part as much as your schedule allows.
